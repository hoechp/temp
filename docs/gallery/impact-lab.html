Motion equation and exact check

q″+2ζq′+q=0, q(0)=1, q′(0)=0, τ=ω₀t. For a 5 Hz natural frequency, τ=12 corresponds to about 0.382 s. The implicit-midpoint step is h=0.05; the screen displays a subset of those steps. E=(q²+v²)/2 satisfies ΔE=−2ζh[(vnext+v)/2]² exactly in rational arithmetic, including the epsilon coefficient. A single linear mode omits forcing, nonlinear friction, multiple modes and feedback delay. The two state slots are an explicit encoding of q and v; algebra generators are not spatial directions.
